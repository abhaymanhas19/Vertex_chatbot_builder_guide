ACME PRIVATE KNOWLEDGE BASE — WEBHOOK DELIVERY
Classification: Internal training sample
Last updated: 2026-09-11

Webhook deliveries are considered successful for HTTP status codes 200 through 299.

For retryable failures, AcmeDesk retries delivery using increasing delays for up to 24 hours. A destination that continually fails may be temporarily disabled.

Webhook consumers should verify event IDs and process them idempotently because the same event can be delivered more than once. Consumers should return a success response quickly and perform slow processing asynchronously.

A 410 response is treated as a signal that the destination is intentionally gone and may cause the subscription to be disabled without the full retry window.
