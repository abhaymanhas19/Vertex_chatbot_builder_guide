ACME PRIVATE KNOWLEDGE BASE — SSO TROUBLESHOOTING
Classification: Internal training sample
Last updated: 2026-09-20

Common SSO symptoms and checks:

1. “Invalid audience” usually means the identity provider is sending an audience value that does not exactly match the AcmeDesk service-provider entity ID shown in Admin > Security > SSO.
2. “Signature validation failed” usually indicates that the active signing certificate in AcmeDesk does not match the certificate used by the identity provider. Upload the current public signing certificate and retry.
3. Login loops can occur when the NameID or primary email claim does not map to an existing AcmeDesk user.
4. Clock-skew errors can occur when assertion timestamps are more than five minutes away from the expected time window.

Before changing production SSO settings, export the current configuration. For Enterprise customers with a Severity 1 login outage, keep the incident bridge open while changes are tested.
