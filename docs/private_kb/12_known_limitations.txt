Known limitations for this fictional product:
- The search index can take several minutes to reflect newly imported archive content.
- The Agent Console does not support offline mode.
- Bulk exports cannot be generated for more than 24 months in a single request.
- A workspace can have at most 20 active webhook subscriptions.
- Local password reset is not applicable to SSO-only users.

If a requested behavior is not documented in the private knowledge base, support agents should say that the documentation does not provide enough information rather than inventing a policy.
